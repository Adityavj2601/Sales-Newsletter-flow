First month at a glance

One edition every Thursday at 10:00 IST. Each edition opens with industry news and carries two "Our work" cards, rotated so no two weeks feel the same. Tap a card to preview it.

What's placeholder: product names in the cards, brand colours, newsletter names (working titles), the reader "Rohan" at "Skyline Developers" (a sample contact), and the video. Taps on polls and buttons only change this page; in the real build they record the response and alert the sales team.

What's real: every industry number, with its source linked under the story. Where the only source is secondary, the pre-flight panel says so.
